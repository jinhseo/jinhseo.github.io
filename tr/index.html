Jinhwan Seo

Ph.D. Student in School of Computing at KAIST

I am a 3rd-year Ph.D. student in School of Computing at KAIST, advised by Professor Sung-eui Yoon. I received my M.S. from POSTECH and my Bachelor's degree from the University of Sheffield.

Outside of research, I like watching films and reading books.

Research interests:

  1. Long-form video understanding: narrative-level reasoning over films and series.
  2. Implicit meaning in video: interpreting metaphor across different video formats.
  3. Modeling time in video-language models: positional encoding for long and unbounded video.
